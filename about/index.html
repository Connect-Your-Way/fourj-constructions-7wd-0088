About FourJ Constructions

A Dubbo carpenter who turns up and does it right.

FourJ Constructions is a local carpentry company built on straightforward, quality work. Run by Josh, we're a mobile outfit — we bring the tools and the know-how to your place and build the sheds, carports, awnings and pergolas that make a property more useful and more comfortable to live on.

We're licensed, we're local, and we'd rather do one job properly than chase ten. If you want a structure built square, finished well and quoted honestly, you're in the right place.

We treat your place like our own — from the first quote to the final clean-up.
